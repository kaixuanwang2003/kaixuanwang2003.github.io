### Research Interests

I work on **Embodied AI**, with a focus on robot manipulation. Much of my recent research is about scalable infrastructure for robot learning. That covers generating large-scale simulation data and digital assets, and building unified benchmarks and evaluation standards for generalist manipulation policies.

I am also interested in **embodied foundation models** and **vision-language-action (VLA) models** that connect reasoning and visual grounding with dexterous control, and in **reinforcement learning** for manipulation and sim-to-real transfer.

If you're interested in related topics and would like to collaborate, feel free to reach out! You can find my email in the hyperlink on the right panel.
